# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary readers are hiring teams reviewing Kanghyeok Lee for junior frontend, full-stack, and AI developer roles in Sydney or elsewhere in Australia. They need a fast route from project proof to the owner's specific contribution, résumé, and contact details.

## Product Purpose

Present working software and give readers enough evidence to discuss how it was designed, built, and tested. The portfolio includes IncidentDesk, Leakly, TailorCV, Job Application Tracker, and the Even & Mark redesign concept.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Retain the five projects, their original roles and factual outcomes, live project links, the résumé and its A4 print layout, and the current email and professional links. Project evidence includes screenshots of the actual interfaces and the IncidentDesk guest demo with sample data. Keep unsupported metrics and testimonials out of the site.

## Product Principles

- Show the interface and what the owner built.
- Make the project, contribution, demo, and contact easy to find.
- Keep content and navigation available with keyboard, narrow screens, and reduced motion.
